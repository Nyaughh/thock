T H O C K

Free and open source, for Android

Your phone is a mechanical keyboard.

Thock turns a phone or tablet into a full-screen 75% keyboard and trackpad. It pairs over Bluetooth like a real one, so there is nothing to install on the computer.

Download for Android

Android 9 or newer

This is the real keyboard, not a picture. Press a key, with your sound on.

BT

Pairs like a real keyboard

The computer sees a Bluetooth keyboard and mouse. No program to run, no driver, no account, and nothing to keep updated on the computer.

13

Sounds like the real thing

Thirteen switches, all recordings of real ones: Black Ink, Cream, Holy Panda, Topre, MX Blue, buckling spring and more. Every row has its own sound, and so does the space bar.

RGB

Looks like your board

Colourways after the Aula F75 and Rainy75, or your own three colours. Six keycap profiles, a white or black case, lit legends and light under the keys.

75%

All the keys, and a trackpad

A full 75% layout with a function row, arrows and an Fn layer for media keys. One key swaps the whole screen for a trackpad with tap, scroll and drag.

Pick a look

Pair it in a minute

  1. 1

    Download thock.apk on the phone and open it to install. Android asks once to allow installing from your browser.

  2. 2

    Open Thock and allow the Nearby devices permission.

  3. 3

    Tap Make this phone visible.

  4. 4

    On the computer, open Bluetooth settings, add a device, and pick the phone.

Next time, open Thock and it reconnects to the last computer on its own. Android only lets an app be a keyboard while it is on screen, so leaving Thock disconnects it.

    On an iPhone or iPad?
